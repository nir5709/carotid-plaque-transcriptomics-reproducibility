# Third-party resources

This repository contains author-written analysis code, locked author-defined signatures, and selected derived tables needed to reconstruct manuscript statistics.

## CIBERSORT / LM22

The manuscript includes CIBERSORT/LM22 analyses. CIBERSORT and the LM22 signature matrix are third-party resources with their own terms of use. This repository therefore does **not** redistribute:

- CIBERSORT source code or packaged implementations;
- `LM22.txt` or any copy of the LM22 signature matrix;
- an installer, mirror, or executable wrapper that downloads or runs a third-party CIBERSORT implementation;
- raw CIBERSORT cell-fraction output matrices.

Only small author-derived summary tables from the historical manuscript run are retained under `data/frozen/`. Those tables contain correlation/test summaries used for manuscript regression and do not reproduce the LM22 matrix or CIBERSORT implementation.

Users who wish to perform a new CIBERSORT analysis should obtain the software and required resources directly from the authorized provider and comply with the applicable terms. The frozen manuscript summaries are provided for result provenance and numerical regression only.

## General rule

Third-party software, reference matrices, databases and other resources are not redistributed unless their terms clearly permit redistribution. Repository documentation may identify such resources and their role in the analysis without copying the resource itself.
